Onra Admin Prototype Update and Walkthrough
Overall modules done 16/16
This update covers these modules:
AI Assistant
Schedule
Staff & Shift
Point of Sale
Attendees
Embed Website

Note:
A new brand colour (teal) now runs across the whole app, and "positive" things — the Present button, the Active / Completed / Attended / Paid badges, the up-trend numbers, and the Class tag — use it instead of the old green, while errors stay red and warnings stay amber. The page background behind the white cards is now a single setting, so it and the sidebar stay one seamless warm shade. The AI Assistant is fully rebranded with an updated logo and a subtle shimmer, and its selected chat is now easy to see. Cancelling a Private or Recovery appointment now uses the same confirmation pop-up as a class, always refunds the customer — and the credit truly returns to their balance — and uses a trash icon everywhere. A new Embed Website tool lets a studio drop its live class schedule and booking flow straight onto its own site. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 AI Assistant
The in-app assistant, fully rebranded with a fresh look.
Capabilities:
New teal look — The assistant's glowing orb, chat bubbles, buttons, and charts now use the new brand colour.
Updated logo — The assistant shows the updated Onra branding logo.
Shimmer effect — The assistant button now shimmers, and it shows a shimmer while it's thinking.
Clearer selected chat — The chat you're viewing in the menu now has a soft mint highlight, so it's obvious which one is open — it used to be almost invisible.

2 Schedule
The class calendar, with a cleaner cancel flow for every session type.
Capabilities:
Confirm before cancelling an appointment — Cancelling a Private or Recovery appointment now opens a confirmation pop-up, just like cancelling a class, from the list, day, week and month views and the quick action bar — instead of jumping to the full details page.
No refund toggle — The refund switch is gone from the cancel pop-up; when the studio cancels, the customer is always refunded automatically.
Trash icon everywhere — The cancel icon is now a trash can across the app — it used to be a "no-entry" icon on the Private / Recovery side.
Refund really returns the credit — A cancelled class now actually returns the credit to the customer's usable balance, not just marks it as refunded.

3 Staff & Shift
Adding a staff member, with clearer rules.
Capabilities:
Password hint — The Temporary password field now shows "At least 8 characters.", and the app requires 8 or more — the hint turns red while it's too short.
No Owner in the role list — Adding a new staff member no longer offers "Owner" as a selectable role.

4 Point of Sale
The front-desk till, with clearer checkout guidance.
Capabilities:
Clear "choose a customer" prompt — With items in the cart but no customer selected, checkout now shows a clear "choose a customer" message instead of a dead greyed-out button.

5 Attendees
The check-in app for attendance staff.
Capabilities:
Real instructor photo in details — The attendee details view now shows the instructor's real photo, with their initials as a fallback if they don't have one.

6 Embed Website
A new tool to put your live schedule and booking on your own website.
Capabilities:
Embed setup in Branding — A new "Embed website" screen gives you copy-paste embed code, a schedule-window setting, and a plain-English how-to guide.
Public schedule widget — Your live class schedule appears on your own site, styled to match your brand.
In-place booking — Visitors log in, book, and reach a "booking confirmed" screen without ever leaving the embed; every booking writes straight through to the admin and customer sides.
Full classes and guests — A full class automatically offers the waitlist, and guests who book through the embed are saved as leads for follow-up.

General updates
New brand colour (rolling out) — A new teal brand colour now runs across admin, instructor, attendee, customer, and the embed widget — no old green left anywhere.
Brand colour for positive states — The Present button, the Active / Completed / Attended / Paid badges, up-trend numbers, and the Class tag now use the brand colour; errors stay red and warnings stay amber.
One background setting — The background behind the white cards is a single setting — change it once and every screen, plus the sidebar, updates to the same warm shade.
