1 Earnings
Your own earnings — pay rate, totals, breakdown and per-booking figures.
Capabilities:
Matches the admin's payroll — Your Earnings now use the same pay-rate setup and calculation as the studio's Staff Payroll Details, so your total earnings and breakdown line up exactly with what the studio sees.
Total earnings breakdown — Total earnings splits across your default rate, pay per class, pay per private and any sales commission, in the same collapsible cards the admin uses.
Per-booking earnings by rate — Each class or private shows the rate that applies to it and what it earned; anything covered by your base salary shows "-" and AED 0, since salary isn't paid per booking.
Clearer Pay rate card — The Pay rate card shows your default rate and its amount at a glance.
