Onra Customer Prototype Update and Walkthrough
Customer modules touched: Home, Membership, Products
This update covers these modules:
Home
Membership
Products

Note:
This update makes cancelling a membership clear and simple, and tidies up gift cards. Cancelling now stops your renewal but keeps your access until the end of the period you've already paid for — no money moves — and whether you can cancel yourself follows the studio's setting. Past bookings show the rating you gave, the gift card details and information now live on one screen with clearer wording, and gift cards and referrals pick up the newest brand colours. In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Home
Your studio home — your next booking, your credits, and a quick way to book.
Capabilities:
Past bookings show your rating — In Past bookings, a class you've rated now shows the rating you gave instead of just an "Attended" status.

2 Membership
Your plan and how it renews.
Capabilities:
Cancel keeps your access — Cancelling a membership stops the renewal, but you keep full access until the end of the period you've paid for, and no money moves.
Follows the studio's setting — Whether you can cancel yourself follows the studio's setting; when it's on, cancelling asks for a reason and your confirmation before it ends.

3 Products
Buy memberships, packages and gift cards, and refer friends.
Capabilities:
One gift card screen — The gift card details and the gift card information sheet are now a single screen, with clearer wording on the amount that the banner used to make confusing.
Clearer custom amount — The custom-amount option in the gift card list reads more clearly.
Fresh brand colours — The gift card illustration and the gift card and referral feature icons now use the newest brand colours.
