Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Customers
This update makes each customer's record show their own real details and tightens the delete rules.
Capabilities:
Saved cards per customer — The Payments tab now shows that customer's own saved cards, with a clean "No saved cards" state when there are none.
Complimentary credits — Granting or removing free credits now updates the balance correctly and records who did it.
Safer delete rules — A customer with any history (bookings, appointments, or payments) can only be archived; a brand-new one can still be deleted.

2 Products
Covers Classes, Private & Recovery sessions, Memberships & Packages, Retail, and Gift Cards.
Capabilities:
New sessions save correctly — Creating a private or recovery session now saves in the right state, so Edit, Deactivate, and Archive work on it right away.
Retired plans stay out of pickers — Archived memberships and packages no longer appear when choosing which plans apply to a class.
Right action for the situation — A product that's already been sold shows "Deactivate"; only a never-sold product shows "Delete."
Gift cards are protected — Trying to delete a gift card that's already been issued now correctly stops and asks you to archive instead.

3 Staff & Payroll
Focuses on making staff actions work end-to-end and paying instructors on real activity.
Capabilities:
Remove staff cleanly — You can now delete a staff member who has no history, and the owner account is always protected.
Real performance charts — A staff member's Overview charts now show that person's actual classes, bookings, and attendance.
Pay follows real attendance — Per-attendee pay now counts who was marked Present, not just who booked.
Consistent contact details — Staff emails now match across the app.

4 Schedule
Makes the class-creation screen and filters reflect your live instructors.
Capabilities:
Real instructor ratings — The class-create screen shows each instructor's actual average rating and review count, or "No ratings yet."
Live instructor filter — The schedule's instructor filter lists your current instructors, including newly added ones.

5 Dashboard
Confirms every figure is real and fixes the today view.
Capabilities:
Today means today — The "Today's sessions" list now shows the actual day's sessions, matching the cards above it.
Real numbers everywhere — Every tile, chart, and pop-up reads live from your real data.

6 Analytics & Reports
Verifies the reports run on real data and fixes a couple of filters.
Capabilities:
Runs on real data — The reports, refund calculations, and Excel/CSV exports all use your live data.
Location filter fixes — The Insights "Referrals" and "Win-back" figures now respond to the Location filter like every other tile, and a placeholder trend was removed.

7 Settings
Turns two settings into rules the app actually follows.
Capabilities:
Cancellation policy now applies — When a member cancels a class, the app uses your configured free-cancel window and charges the late-cancel fee you set, instead of a fixed rule.
Referral rewards for both sides — The referred friend now receives their reward on first purchase, and the monthly referral budget cap is respected.
Correct branch details — The Locations list now shows each branch's real email.

8 Marketing
Tightens campaign rules and keeps edits accurate.
Capabilities:
Smarter delete rules — A campaign that's already been sent can only be archived, keeping its stats, not deleted.
Send-from-edit records results — Sending a saved draft now records its send, open, and click figures like a fresh send.
Edits keep their details — Editing a campaign no longer loses its content type or original date.

General updates
Consistent "Archived" section — Across the admin (Customers, Staff, Plans & packages, Class types, Retail, Locations, Promo codes, Marketing, Announcements, Agreements, Services, Tax, Gift cards, Pay rates), archived items now tuck into a collapsible "Archived" section so your active list stays clean.
Clear archive-vs-delete rules — Roles and Shifts are delete-only, scheduled classes are cancel-only, and anything with history can only be archived (never permanently deleted). A pay rate that's in use asks you to reassign staff before archiving it.
Filters reset between modules — Opening a new module starts with a clean filter.
