Note:
This update moves marketing spend onto your campaigns. You now set a Budget when you create a campaign, and it flows straight into the Acquisition Efficiency report alongside your referral program budget — so the separate Marketing Spend page is no longer needed and has been hidden. Every spend, CPL, CAC and ROAS figure now matches across the report, Insights, the dashboard and the in-app assistant. We also tidied every page's web address to match the menu (old links still work automatically), aligned your lead source names across leads, spend and reports, added empty states on the dashboard, and fixed a loading glitch on a few pages. Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Marketing
Marketing spend now lives on your campaigns instead of a separate page.
Capabilities:
Budget on the campaign — When you create or edit a campaign you set a Budget, and it's shown again on the campaign's detail page so the number always lives in one place.
Referral budget counts too — Your referral program's monthly budget is included automatically as its own spend line — no extra entry needed.
Marketing Spend page removed — The separate Marketing Spend page is hidden; spend is captured on campaigns and referral now, so there's nothing to maintain in two places.

2 Reports & Insights
The Acquisition Efficiency report now reads real spend, and every spend figure across the app agrees.
Capabilities:
Acquisition Efficiency runs on real spend — The report pulls spend from your campaign budgets — shared across each campaign's channels based on how many messages each one sent — plus your referral budget. Your report columns are unchanged.
The channels that matter — Spend rows are your actual channels: Email, WhatsApp, SMS and Referral. WhatsApp and Referral also bring in new leads, so their cost-per-lead and cost-per-acquisition fill in.
Every number agrees — Spend, CPL, CAC and ROAS now match across the Acquisition Efficiency report, Insights, the dashboard's marketing widget and the export, and the in-app assistant reports the same figures. No more conflicting numbers.

3 Lead Lifecycle
Lead sources now speak the same language everywhere.
Capabilities:
Aligned source names — The Lead Lifecycle source names match the lead tags and the marketing spend channels, so leads, spend and reports all line up.
Connected to reports — The Lead Lifecycle list and the reports read the same source data, so what you see in one matches the other.

4 Dashboard
Empty states on the two main dashboard cards.
Capabilities:
Clear empty states — "Today's sessions" and "Recent activity" now show a friendly "nothing yet" message instead of a blank box when there's no data for the day.

General updates
Web addresses match the menu — Six pages moved so each page's web address matches where it sits in the menu — Payroll under Staff, Referrals and Promotions under Marketing, Classes, Private and Recovery under Products, and instructor class and earnings detail under Instructor. Old links redirect automatically, so nothing breaks.
No broken links — Every link across the app was checked after the move — menu, header, breadcrumbs, the in-app assistant and page-to-page links — so there are no dead ends.
Numbers line up in tables — In every admin table, number-only columns are now right-aligned so figures are easy to scan and compare.
Smoother page loads — Fixed an issue where a few pages briefly showed an error when opened fresh; they now load cleanly.
