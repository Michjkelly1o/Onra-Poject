Note:
Every number on the Dashboard is now clickable — on the Today, Coming Up, and Performance tabs, each KPI opens a pop-up listing the exact records behind it, always filtered to match the card. Class templates and categories are merged into a single "Class" menu under Products, and the products menu is reordered. The schedule's data was audited and fixed so no instructor is scheduled during time off and nothing is double-booked. Point of Sale gets a taller cart, simpler discounts, and more accurate per-branch stock and refunds. Booking Rules gains a cleaner Guest Bookings panel with a guest limit, and guests you add during booking now become leads automatically. The AI Assistant moves to the top header with a refreshed look, and a new visual style keeps rolling out across the app. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Dashboard
The owner's home view — now every number opens the details behind it.
Capabilities:
Click any metric — On the Today, Coming Up, and Performance tabs, every KPI card opens a pop-up listing the exact records behind the number — the sales, customers, bookings, or plans that make it up.
Numbers always match their list — Each pop-up is built the same way as the card it comes from, so the total you click always equals the list you see.
Filters carry through — Changing the location, the session type (Class / Private / Recovery), or the date period updates both the card and its pop-up together.
Capacity used, by day — In Coming Up, the Capacity used section now shows the date under each bar, matching the Revenue section.
Click to filter — Clicking a session type in the Capacity used section opens the schedule filtered to that type for that day.

2 Schedule
The class calendar, with its underlying data cleaned up.
Capabilities:
No classes during time off — We audited and fixed every case where an instructor had an upcoming class on a day they're on time off; those conflicts are gone.
No double-booking — Fixed sessions that were booked at the same time on the same instructor or room, across every session type.
Cleaner list view — The "Flexible" badge was removed from the class list view to match the rest of the schedule.

3 Products
The products area, reorganized so everything a member buys sits in one place.
Capabilities:
One "Class" menu — Class templates and Class categories are now a single "Class" item with tabs, laid out like Memberships & packages and moved under Products.
New menu order — The Products menu now reads Class, Private, Recovery, Memberships & packages, Retail, Gift cards.
Shorter intro package name — The intro package label was simplified — the word "class" was dropped so the name stays short.

4 Point of Sale
The front-desk till, tidier and more accurate on stock.
Capabilities:
Taller cart — The cart now fills the available screen height, so there's less scrolling during a sale.
Simpler discounts — Promotion and Custom discount each use one field to apply or cancel, with the separate "applied" box removed.
More compact payment — The "Detail payment" title is gone and the divider now sits above the payment section for a shorter layout.
Sold from the right branch — A sale now counts against the branch where it happens, not the customer's home branch, so revenue and stock land in the correct place.
Accurate out-of-stock — The out-of-stock check uses the selling branch's own units, so a branch that's actually empty can't be oversold.
Safer refunds — Refunds only apply their effects once the status actually changes, avoiding accidental double-adjustments.

5 Settings
Studio-wide configuration, with a cleaner Guest Bookings setup.
Capabilities:
Guest Bookings as a panel — In Booking Rules, Guest Bookings now opens in a Customize side panel — the same pattern as Waitlist and Booking Window — and the main page shows it simply as Enabled or Disabled.
Guest booking limit — You can now cap how many guest bookings are allowed, set with the same toggle-and-number control used elsewhere.
Consistent wording — "Members can use their plans for guests" now reads "Customers can use their plans for guests", matching the rest of the app.
Guests become leads — Adding a guest during booking now creates that guest as a Lead in the Customer module automatically, so you can follow up later.

6 Reports
Sales and stock reports, made more accurate.
Capabilities:
Honest refunds — The Retail Sales report never rewrites past months; a refund shows as its own line in the period it actually happens.
Cleaner stock report — Stock on Hand dropped a misleading Location column that made the numbers harder to read.

7 Staff & Shift
The staff and shift screens, now scrolling like the schedule.
Capabilities:
Matching scroll — Every tab in Staff and Shift now scrolls the same way as the Schedule — the page and the list scroll together, and the pagination stays pinned in place.

8 Attendees
The check-in app for attendance staff.
Capabilities:
Clearer class card — Each class card now shows the instructor's photo and name, plus a quick row of spots, room, and who the class is open to.

9 AI Assistant
The in-app assistant, in a new home with a fresh look.
Capabilities:
Now in the header — The assistant button moved from floating at the bottom to the top header, just before Notifications.
New look — It uses the latest design with the updated Onra icon and brand colour.

General updates
Refreshed look (rolling out) — The new visual style keeps rolling out across the app — buttons, colours, icons, and surfaces.
Cleaner sample data — Every customer, staff member, and lead now has a unique name, email, and phone — no more duplicate names across the sample data.
Tighter layouts — With the assistant moved to the top, extra bottom spacing was removed across screens, and each page now either fills the screen or hugs its content as appropriate.
