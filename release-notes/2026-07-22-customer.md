Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Membership Freeze
Pause a membership from the member's own account.
Capabilities:
Corrected freeze behaviour — Freezing now follows the studio's policy correctly — the right fee, the right resume date, and bookings blocked while paused — and stays in step with what staff see on the admin side.

2 Products & Plans
Browse memberships and class packs available to buy.
Capabilities:
Per-class price — Class packs now show the per-class cost next to the price, e.g. "AED 1390 · AED 139/class", so the value is clear at a glance.
