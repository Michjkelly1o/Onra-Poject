Note:
This update clarifies your policies and tidies several screens. Booking rules now separate "Booking cancellations & no-shows" from plan policies, and the old "Cancel & freeze plan policy" splits into two clear sections — Plan cancellation and Plan freeze — each with its own side panel. The dashboard reads more honestly: the revenue breakdown says "Earned", and Coming up swaps the old capacity figure for a Class Utilization heatmap by time of day, plus a Bookings heatmap for privates and recovery. Product menu wording is simpler, the marketing forms now open as side panels, and staff pay is "Pay per private" everywhere. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Settings
Studio policies and configuration.
Capabilities:
Clearer cancellation policy name — "Cancellation policy" under Booking rules is now "Booking cancellations & no-shows", so it's obvious what it covers.
Plan policies split in two — The old "Cancel & freeze plan policy" is now two separate sections — Plan cancellation and Plan freeze — each with its own side panel and its own wording.

2 Products
Memberships, packages and class products.
Capabilities:
Simpler menu wording — The product sub-menu now reads "Classes" instead of "Class", and "Plans" in place of "Memberships & Packages".

3 Staff & Payroll
Staff shifts, schedules and pay rates.
Capabilities:
"Pay per private" everywhere — The "Pay per appointment" pay rate is now "Pay per private", matching the wording used across the rest of the admin.
Deleting a shift looks forward — Removing or unassigning a shift applies from the current week onward and leaves past shifts untouched.
Cleaner shift card — The shift card drops the title and shows just the shift time.
Simpler shift hover — Hovering a shift shows a shorter summary, without the class status.

4 Schedule
The class schedule and attendance.
Capabilities:
Mark present 30 minutes early — You can now mark a customer present up to 30 minutes before the class starts, matching the Attendee view.

5 Dashboard
The studio's at-a-glance numbers.
Capabilities:
Revenue reads "Earned" — The revenue breakdown drops "earned this period" and renames the "recognized" column to "Earned", so it reflects the actual revenue taken.
Class Utilization heatmap — Coming up replaces the old capacity figure with a Class Utilization heatmap split by Morning, Afternoon and Evening, like the one in Performance.
Bookings heatmap for privates and recovery — Privates and recovery now show a Bookings heatmap where a darker shade means more bookings, instead of a capacity percentage.

6 Marketing
Campaigns, announcements and promotions.
Capabilities:
Forms open as side panels — Creating or editing a Campaign, Promotion or Announcement now happens in a side panel instead of a full page, so you stay on your list.

General updates
Archive opens as a pop-up — The archived list now opens in a pop-up modal instead of a separate page.
Consistent side-panel steps — Side-panel forms now use a horizontal stepper to move through their steps.
