Note:
This update sharpens the AI Agent and the customer workflow, and reworks instructor payroll end to end. The agent now searches your leads as well as your customers, looks every answer up in your real data before replying — and says so when it can't find something — and shows clearer messages while importing. Customer Management gets a round of fixes across avatars, filters, reassignment and the activity log. Studio Setup only marks a step done when your data really backs it. Instructor payroll now supports flexible pay-rate setups, with the same numbers across Staff Payroll Details, Run Payroll and the export. A new Retail module is also being introduced — you'll see it take shape over the coming updates. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Customer Management
The studio's customer and lead list, with a full profile behind each one.
Capabilities:
Cleaner customer cards — Cards now show the customer's photo, or clean initials when there's no photo, and the arrow sits inline with the status so each row reads straight.
"Assigned to me" returns your customers — The "Assigned to me" chip now shows the customers assigned to you instead of coming back empty.
Branch filters agree — The toolbar filter and the filter panel no longer clash; picking a branch returns the matching customers instead of an empty list.
Reassigning moves the work too — Reassign a customer to a new owner and their open follow-up tasks move across with them.
"Reached out" advances a lead — Marking a lead "Reached out" now moves it to Contacted, as expected.
Activity log names the real change — The log now says what actually changed instead of always reading "Edited customer profile".

2 AI Agent
A chat assistant that helps you find answers, bring in your existing data, and set up the studio.
Capabilities:
Searches your leads too — Ask about a lead and the agent finds it, searching your leads alongside your customers.
Looks it up, never guesses — For any question about a specific record the agent checks your real data first, and tells you when it can't find something instead of inventing an answer. This now covers every kind of record — customers, memberships, classes, staff and more.
Matches the admin's view — For the same customer, the agent shows the same lifecycle stage as the rest of the admin.
Lead results don't dead-end — Lead rows in a result aren't clickable, so a lead search never lands on a "not found" page.
Clearer, complete import messages — During a data import, confusing "0 imported" notes are replaced with clear next steps, and every message now shows properly instead of appearing blank.

3 Studio Setup
The guided checklist that tracks whether the studio is ready to go.
Capabilities:
Steps reflect your real data — A setup step now shows as done only when your studio data truly backs it — no more false "all done".
Tighter referral and cancellation checks — The referral and cancellation-policy steps no longer show green by default; they turn green only once they're really set up.

4 Retail
A new module for selling retail products, being introduced now.
Capabilities:
Retail categories — Set up the categories that organise your retail products. The catalog, POS checkout and retail reports are being built next, so parts of this module are still in progress.

5 Payroll
Instructor and staff compensation — pay rates, earnings, and payroll runs.
Capabilities:
Flexible instructor pay rates — Instructors can be paid on any valid mix of a Default (base salary), Pay per class and Pay per private; other staff keep the simple Default pay rate.
Safe pay-rate rules — At least one pay rate always stays on, and switching off the Default automatically keeps both Pay per class and Pay per private enabled — so an instructor is never left with no way to be paid.
Bookings show only per-booking earnings — In Total bookings, a class or private shows its rate and earnings only when that per-booking rate is switched on; anything covered by the base salary shows "-" and AED 0.
Complete earnings breakdown — Total earnings adds up every enabled pay rate plus any sales commission, with the base salary shown only in the breakdown — never doubled onto each booking.
Same numbers everywhere — Staff Payroll Details, the earnings breakdown, Total bookings, Run Payroll and the exported payroll sheet all use one calculation, so the figures always match — and the export reflects the latest pay-rate setup.
Simpler pay-rate summary — The staff payroll side panel and the Pay rate card keep it general, showing just the default pay rate.
