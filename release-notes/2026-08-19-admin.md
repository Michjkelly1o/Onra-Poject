1 Reports
The reports have been reworked to match your updated spec — cleaner defaults, exact naming, new columns with real data, and the fixes from your comments.
Capabilities:
Cleaner default view — Each report opens showing only the key columns; the rest sit in the "Columns" picker so studios aren't overwhelmed. Pick what you want to see and it sticks next time you open the report.
Names match your spec — Columns, report titles, and report groups renamed to your wording — groups are now Plans, Customers, Bookings & Attendance, Staff and Inventory; columns like Item, Sale category, Amount Due / Amount Paid, Net sales (before tax), Total spend, Rebooked and Session Name.
Removed the columns you flagged — Status, Avg visits, Attendance rate %, Avg show-up %, Engagement status, Attribution window and Stock turnover are gone.
Location after date — On the 16 reports you selected, the Location column now sits right after the Date column.
New columns with real data — Staff Name (Sales, Refunds, Discounts, Plan Changes, Retail Sales), Category on Bookings, Reason on Frozen Plans, Months with us on Customer Data, Average months before leaving on Member Movement, and Sessions taught / Covered by someone / Covered for others on Hours & Sessions.
Refunds report covers everything — Refunds now include every product sold at the point of sale — memberships, packages, private and recovery sessions, retail and gift cards — not just plans.
Your comments applied — Recovered now shows Y / N; unlimited plans show "Unlimited" instead of 0 credits; the Days frozen total shows an average, not a sum; sell-through shows "—" when nothing came in that period; and the ordering tweaks you asked for (Gift card # first, Class date first, Top Classes ranked by highest bookings).
Checked against your spec — Every column, default, name and calculation was verified to match your file.

2 Customer Management
The refund flow inside a customer's Payment history is clearer and now covers every product, and the archive views were tidied.
Capabilities:
Refund covers all products — You can refund any item from a customer's payment history — memberships, packages, private and recovery sessions, retail and gift cards.
Clearer refund window — The refund pop-up shows what was paid and where the money goes, and refunds return to the original payment method automatically.
Refund reason — When refunding you pick a reason from a set list (with a required note for "Other"), so every refund records why it happened.
Calendar icon for sessions — In Payment history, private and recovery sessions now use a calendar icon instead of the box icon.
Cleaner booking views — Booking history no longer lists "upcoming" bookings (those live on the Overview), and the Overview's upcoming booking now shows the date, not just the time.
Tidier archive pop-up — The archive customer pop-up has clearer wording and the reason field spacing is fixed.
Easier archived list — The customer table in the Archived view now fills the width, so the 3-dots actions are visible without scrolling.

3 Staff & Schedule
Shift edits are safer and the schedule reads more cleanly.
Capabilities:
Full shift time labels — The time on each shift in the staff schedule now shows in full, no longer cut off.
Deletes respect the past — Deleting a shift, or a shift template, applies from the current week forward — past shifts are left untouched.
Unassign confirmation — Unassigning a shift now asks how far forward to apply it, via a period dropdown in the confirmation.

4 Settings
A small tidy-up to how tax rates are added.
Capabilities:
Add tax rate as a side panel — Adding a new tax rate opens as a right-side panel, matching the rest of the app.

5 Marketing
A new place to record marketing spend so the acquisition numbers can be calculated.
Capabilities:
Marketing Spend (in development) — Record ad and marketing spend by channel and month so the Acquisition Efficiency report can work out cost per lead, cost per customer and return on ad spend. This module is still being finished — for now it shows a 404.

General updates
Consistent time format — Times across the app now read as AM / PM with a dot for minutes and no ":00" on whole hours (e.g. 10 AM, 10.30 AM).
Numbers right-aligned — Number-only columns are right-aligned across every table.
Delete only when safe — The bulk "Delete" action appears only when every selected item has no history; anything with history can't be deleted (checked across the admin side, including the Archived views).
Consistent side-panel steps — The step indicator now appears the same way on every side panel.
