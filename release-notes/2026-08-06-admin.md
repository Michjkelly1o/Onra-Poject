Note:
Every class now has a real, qualified instructor behind it, and the person you see in the schedule is always the one you can pick when editing — we tracked down and fixed a mismatch that used to block that. Made-up instructors and a fake class were removed, so the dashboard only shows the studio's real people and classes. Each instructor's Today's Schedule on the Staff page now lays out their shift as time slices and shows all-day time off as plain text, and clicking it jumps straight to that day in the schedule with the instructor already filtered. Booking Rules gains Guest Bookings settings, payroll tax and VAT are now clearly separated (wages vs products), and a refreshed look is starting to roll out across the app. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Schedule
The class calendar, now built on one reliable set of instructor data and a cleaner default view.
Capabilities:
One instructor list everywhere — The schedule list, the edit picker, and the "who can teach this" check now all read the same instructor data, so an instructor who appears in a class can always be selected when editing.
Right instructor on every class — Each class is assigned an instructor who actually teaches that class type, is based at that branch, isn't on time off, and isn't already booked at that time.
Sensible rooms and days — Every class sits in a room that fits its capacity, and nothing is scheduled on a day the branch is closed.
Cleaner default calendar — Day, Week, and Month views now hide Cancelled and Completed classes by default; filter them back in whenever you want to see them.
Completed classes stand out — When you do show them, completed classes are clearly marked so they read differently from active ones.
Edits always hold together — When editing a class, the instructor and start time always stay filled, the correct branch, room, and spot layout are kept, and you can't save without an instructor.
Changes flow through — Renaming or reassigning a staff member updates the schedule automatically, and reassigning a class moves its ratings and attendance to the new instructor.
Checked end to end — All 85 classes, 523 bookings, and 25 ratings were verified across the admin, instructor, customer, and attendee sides with zero errors.

2 Staff
The staff area, tidied so each action opens exactly what you expect.
Capabilities:
Simpler Add button — Under the Staff menu the Add button is now just "+ Add" and opens the Add Staff flow directly, with no nested Add Shift or Add Time Off options.
Today's Schedule as time slices — An instructor's Today's Schedule now shows time slices across their assigned shift — a 7:00 AM–12:00 PM morning shift shows five slots — filled blocks use the branch colour, and empty slots still show when there's nothing booked.
Clear all-day time off — All-day time off now reads as plain text — "Time Off", the reason (e.g. Vacation), and "All day" — instead of a time bar.
Jump to the day — Clicking an instructor's Today's Schedule opens the Schedule in Day View with that instructor already set as the active filter.
Owner row cleaned up — The Owner row now offers View plus a new Account Settings action to edit the Owner's details, and the Owner's information stays consistent everywhere in admin.
No shift step on new staff — Assign Shift was removed from the staff creation flow, since Staff and Shift are separate modules now.
Matching detail actions — The actions inside a staff member's detail page now match the ones available from the Staff table.

3 Shift
The shift planner, with its Add menu scoped to shift work only.
Capabilities:
Focused Add menu — The Add menu now offers only Add Shift and Add Time Off; Add Staff was removed, since staff are managed in their own module.

4 Payroll
Payroll tax, now clearly separated from sales tax.
Capabilities:
Salaries only — The payroll tax tab now lists salaries only — memberships, packages, retail, and gift cards no longer appear there.
Charged on wages — Confirmed that payroll tax applies to staff wages, not to products or services.
Two taxes, kept apart — Payroll tax on salaries and VAT (5%) on products and services are now clearly separated, and Candice's pay-rate mismatch was fixed so payroll figures and notifications match the schedule.

5 Point of Sale
The front-desk till, with a cleaner end to each sale.
Capabilities:
One button at the end — After completing a transaction the receipt shows a single confirmation button instead of a second "Complete transaction", so you never confirm twice.

6 Settings
Studio-wide configuration, now covering guest bookings.
Capabilities:
Guest Bookings — Booking Rules under Operations gains a Guest Bookings section with toggles for "Members can use their plan for guests" and "Allow unlimited plans for guests".

7 Reports
Small naming fix.
Capabilities:
"Sales" report — The report previously called "Total Sales" is now simply "Sales".

8 AI Assistant
The in-app assistant, faster and reading only real studio data.
Capabilities:
Faster replies — The assistant now reuses its saved instructions between messages, so answers come back quicker.
Real data only — An end-to-end check confirmed the assistant reads from the same data as the rest of the app, with no made-up values anywhere.

General updates
Refreshed look (rolling out) — A new visual style is being applied across the app — starting with buttons, colours, and surfaces — and will continue over the coming updates.
