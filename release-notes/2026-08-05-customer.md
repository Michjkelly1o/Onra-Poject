Onra Customer Prototype Update and Walkthrough
Customer modules touched: Home, Search, Class Booking, Checkout & Payment, Products
This update covers these modules:
Home
Search
Class Booking
Checkout & Payment
Products

Note:
This update makes moving through the app feel like one continuous flow. Opening a payment, a promo, or a payment-method list now slides forward and back inside the same panel instead of closing one and popping open another, and success messages always sit in front. Booking a private or recovery session opens on the current week again, rating a class happens in a slide-up panel, checkout leads with your recent payment method, and a few labels around the home screen, search and products read cleaner. In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Home
Your studio home — your next booking, your credits, and a quick way to book.
Capabilities:
Smarter Browse schedule button — The Browse schedule button hides while the page is still and slides in as you scroll down, so it's there when you want it and out of the way when you don't.
Browse from an empty state — When you have no upcoming bookings, the Upcoming section shows a Browse schedule button to get you started.

2 Search
Find and book classes, private sessions and recovery.
Capabilities:
Quieter capacity labels — The spots-left badge is more subtle on group classes and is hidden on private and recovery sessions; group recovery still shows an "Up to X" capacity so you know the size.

3 Class Booking
Book a class and manage a booking.
Capabilities:
Cancel booking scrolls with the page — On a booking's details, the Cancel booking action scrolls with the content instead of sticking to the bottom of the screen.
Rate in a slide-up panel — Rating a class now happens in a slide-up panel instead of a separate page, so you stay in place.
Opens on the current week — Picking a date for a private or recovery session opens on this week with today selected, and the days you can book run from today through the next seven.

4 Checkout & Payment
Pay for a class, a private or recovery session, or a product.
Capabilities:
Your recent payment method first — Checkout shows just your most-recent payment method, with a See more link that opens the full list in a panel.
One continuous panel — The payment method list and the promo screen slide in as forward-and-back steps within the same panel, so it feels like moving between screens, not opening a new sheet each time.
Full promo details — Voucher cards and the promo details are no longer cut off, so you can read the whole offer before applying it.

5 Products
Buy memberships, packages and gift cards.
Capabilities:
Cleaner single-class packages — One-credit packages no longer show a per-class price, since there's only one class in them.

General updates
Panels feel like one flow — Opening another panel from within a panel now slides forward and back like moving between screens, instead of closing one and opening a fresh one on top.
Confirmations always in front — Success messages now appear in front of any open panel, never tucked behind it.
