Onra Admin Prototype Update — Marketing rework

This update reshapes Marketing around what each thing is *for*, per your notes. Marketing is now three clear tools, and bookable events moved to Schedule.

At a glance:
- Campaigns — something to send: a message to a chosen segment of customers.
- Promotions — something to discount: a price on a product, with a validity window. (Unchanged.)
- Announcements — something to say: information with a show-until date. No action, no revenue.
- Events — removed from Marketing. Anything bookable (a date with capacity) lives in Schedule.

Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, and send items to test the workflows. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate.

1 Campaigns — "send a message to a chosen segment"
Campaigns are now an outbound send, not a passive banner.
Capabilities:
- Write once — banner, name, message, a topic, and an optional call to action (Book a class, an external link, or none).
- Choose who receives it — Everyone, by membership, by segment (Lead / Member / Inactive), or a hand-picked list of customers, scoped by branch.
- See the reach before you send — a live "this campaign will reach N customers" count, counting only people who opted into push and this topic.
- Send now, schedule for later, or save as a draft — the list has All / Sent / Scheduled / Drafts tabs.
- Real results — sending a campaign records it, so the Sent / Open rate / Click rate on the campaign, and the Campaign Performance report, Insights, and Dashboard, reflect real sends.
- Delivered to the customer's notifications — a campaign is a push (it lands in the customer's notification inbox), not a home-screen banner.

2 Announcements — "say something"
An announcement is pure information — like "We're closed on April 20".
Capabilities:
- Information only — a banner and a push, with no button and no purchase. It never counts as revenue or a conversion.
- Show-until date — it shows in the app up to that day, then hides on its own.
- Goes to the right people — pushed to customers in the selected branches who opted into Studio announcements, and shown on the customer home "What's on" banner.
- You see the reach — publishing tells you how many customers it was sent to.

3 Promotions — unchanged
Still "a discount on a product, with a validity window" (e.g. a yoga pack, this week only).

4 Events — moved to Schedule
Bookable events (a date with capacity) belong in Schedule, so the Events menu was removed from Marketing.

General
- The customer "What's on" banner now shows Announcements; Campaigns arrive as a push in the notification inbox under a new "Updates" section — keeping the two clearly separate.
- All marketing data stays connected: sends and pushes are consent-aware (a customer only receives a message when they've opted into both the channel and the topic).
