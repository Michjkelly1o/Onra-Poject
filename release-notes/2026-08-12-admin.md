Note:
This update reworks staff scheduling and tightens how marketing and reports read. Shifts and Time Off now live under Staff, the staff schedule shows each person's classes on hover, overlapping shifts are blocked unless you choose to replace one, and every delete now asks first. A new Operations setting controls who can cancel a membership. Marketing gets a real overhaul: each campaign now picks a content type that decides its channels, the "will reach" number only counts members who actually opted in, and every send is recorded per channel so Reports, Insights and the dashboard all match. Reports themselves read more honestly — refunds show as negatives in red brackets, every report carries a Location column, and "Sales by Category" and "Sales by Item" merge into one "Sales Breakdown". In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Staff & Shift
Staff shifts, schedules and pay rates, all in one place.
Capabilities:
Shifts and Time Off under Staff — Shifts and Time Off have moved out of the Schedule module and now sit under Staff, so all staff planning is in one place.
Staff schedule shows classes — The staff schedule shows each person's shifts and classes as bars; hover a bar to see the full schedule details.
Multiple shifts, no accidental clashes — A staff member can hold several shifts, but two can't overlap unless you deliberately choose to replace the conflicting one, and duplicate-shift assignment is fixed.
Smarter shift confirmation — The period confirmation only appears for recurring shifts now — a single shift saves without it — and it uses a Weeks/Month dropdown to set how far ahead.
Delete always confirms — Every delete shows a confirmation first, shifts included, which used to delete on the spot.
"Shift Assigned" column — The staff table's "Today's Schedule" column is now "Shift Assigned".
Accurate totals, cleaner list — Shift and time-off totals add up correctly, and the filter on the shift sub-menu is removed.
Pay rate toggle and brand-colour cards — The pay-rate setup uses a clearer on/off toggle, and schedule cards use the newest brand colours.

2 Time Off
Record and track staff time off.
Capabilities:
Simpler Time Off table — Columns are now Staff, Date & Time Off, and Note; Range and Reason no longer clutter the list.
Reason kept in the form — You still choose a reason when logging time off — Annual Leave, Sick, Personal, Training, Religious Leave, or Other — it's just hidden from the table.
Time-off metrics — A metrics strip on the Time Off tab shows Today, This Week and This Month at a glance.
Consistent "Time Off" label — The calendar's time-off block now reads "Time Off" to match the rest of the app.

3 Operations
Studio-wide policy settings.
Capabilities:
Cancel-membership control — A new setting decides who can cancel a membership — off by default, so members cancel their own; turn it on to require a reason and the customer's confirmation.
Clear end-of-access message — When a membership is cancelled, the confirmation reads "Your membership ends 24 Aug. You keep full access until then."

4 Products
Memberships, packages and class products.
Capabilities:
"Single class" pack — The old intro pack is now called "Single class" and covers 2 or 3 classes.

5 Marketing
Campaigns, announcements and promotions sent to members.
Capabilities:
Content type picker — Creating a campaign, you choose New class launch or Special offers, and that choice decides which channels — Email / WhatsApp / SMS — it goes out on.
Honest reach count — "Will reach N" now counts only members who opted in to that topic and at least one channel, not everyone.
Channels shown everywhere — The reach box, the "sent" toast, and the campaign detail all show the channels used, e.g. "sent to 919 customers via Push, Email, WhatsApp".
Announce a promo — Promotions have an "Announce to customers" toggle; switch it on to send the promo as a Promo code offers message, and the promo detail shows whether it was announced.
Announcements stay consistent — Announcements always send as Studio announcements with the same opt-in-aware counting, and editing an announced promo keeps its original date so it isn't re-sent as new.
Reaches the member's bell — Announced promos appear in the member's notifications as their own row with a ticket icon, and the Updates feed only shows items a member opted in to.
Reports always match — One performance record is saved per channel, so Reports, Insights and the dashboard widgets reflect exactly what was sent.
Tidier forms and detail — The marketing menu was audited across all five areas with shorter helper text, and "Valid until" no longer shows on campaigns, which don't expire.

6 Reports
Studio reports and exports.
Capabilities:
Plain variance wording — Every report's comparison line now reads "vs previous day / week / month / quarter / year" instead of YoY/MoM abbreviations.
Refunds read correctly — Refunds show as negatives in the Payments report; a voided sale is removed, while a refunded sale shows the charge plus a separate negative refund.
Negatives in red brackets — Negative amounts show as (AED 170) in red across every report and the Excel export.
Location on every report — All 16 reports carry a Location column so each row shows its studio when viewing all locations, and online purchases show a proper location instead of a blank.
Stock per studio — Stock on Hand is tracked per studio, one row per product per location.
Real discount data — The Discounts and Promotion Redemptions reports now use real data.
Sales Breakdown — "Sales by Category" and "Sales by Item" are merged into one "Sales Breakdown"; it totals each item into a single row, groups by Item or Item Type, and the duplicate Memberships/Packages categories are fixed.

7 Dashboard
The studio's at-a-glance numbers.
Capabilities:
Revenue reads "Earned" — Opening the revenue tile now says Earned, not accrued, since it's the actual revenue taken.
