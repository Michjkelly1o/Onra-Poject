Note:
This update tidies up booking details and payment across the app so everything reads the same way. Class and appointment booking details now share one structure — a "Book to" section and a "Payment details" section, separated by dividers, for both your own and guest bookings — and cancelled bookings put those sections before the refund. Guests can now pay with your plan when your booking rules allow it, the waitlist shows the same "Pay with" details as a normal booking, and gift cards flow forward and back in one panel like the rest of the app. Behind the scenes we audited tax, credit packages, and the overall credit balance so the numbers always line up. In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Home
Your studio home — your next booking, your credits, and a quick way to book.
Capabilities:
Rated bookings stay put — In Previous bookings, a session stays visible after you've rated it instead of disappearing, so you keep a record of what you attended.

2 Class Booking
Book a class and manage a booking.
Capabilities:
Pay with your plan for a guest — Guest bookings now offer "Use my plan" as a payment option when your booking rules allow it, so you can cover a guest with your own plan.
Guest details in "Book to" — A booking's details now show the Guest Information card right under "Book to", with clearer wording on what happens if a guest booking is cancelled.
Clear "Book to" and "Payment details" — The two sections are separated by dividers and always show the "Pay with" method, so you can see who the booking is for and how it's paid at a glance.
Credits left for the right package — When you hold more than one credit package, Review & Book counts "Credits left after this booking" against the exact package you're using, not your total balance.

3 Appointments
Book and manage a private or recovery session.
Capabilities:
Payment details on appointments — An appointment's details now include a Payment details section showing the appointment name, the total, and the payment method — matching how class bookings read.

4 Waitlist
Join the waitlist for a full class.
Capabilities:
"Pay with" on the waitlist — Joining a waitlist now shows the same "Pay with" section as booking a class, for both your own and guest entries, so payment is set before a spot opens.

5 Checkout & Payment
Pay for a class, an appointment, or a product.
Capabilities:
Gift cards flow in one panel — Adding a gift card to the cart or paying for it directly now opens the Gift Card Information step with a Back button, sliding forward and back in the same panel instead of closing and reopening a new one.
Tax always matches the studio — The payment flow now uses the same tax setup configured on the studio side, so the tax you're charged is always correct.

6 Products
Buy memberships, packages and gift cards.
Capabilities:
Custom gift card shows its range — The custom-amount gift card now shows the amount you can choose — for example, from AED 100 to AED 2,000 — so it's clear what's allowed before you enter a value.
Cleaner Gift Cards and Retail tabs — The Active Plan card no longer appears on the Gift Cards and Retail tabs, where it didn't belong.

7 My Plan
Your plan, credits, and saved gift cards.
Capabilities:
Correct credit package price — A credit package no longer shows as AED 0; it displays the real price you paid.
Credit balance that adds up — Each package's remaining credits and progress bar now always match your overall credit balance, so the numbers line up wherever you look.

General updates
One structure for booking details — Class and appointment booking details now share the same layout — a "Book to" section and a "Payment details" section — for both your own and guest bookings.
Cancelled bookings read in order — On a cancelled booking, "Book to" and "Payment details" now sit before "Refund details", with every section separated by dividers.
Full booking and payment audit — Booking logic, payment details, tax, credit packages, credit balance, guest information, and booking-detail layouts were reviewed end to end to keep everything consistent across the app.
