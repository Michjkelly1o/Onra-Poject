Note:
This update teaches the AI Agent far more of your studio — class management, staff, studio settings and customer plans & referrals — adds a "what's interesting" scan that surfaces the biggest signals right now, spreads broad questions across eight topics, and guides a brand-new studio when there's no data yet. The new Retail module gains a full product create/edit form with a live preview, plus a product detail page showing stock by branch and recent activity. Staff creation moves into a side panel, and Referral, Booking Rules and Customer Notifications get cleaner, full-width layouts — alongside a round of Customer Management fixes. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 AI Agent
A chat assistant that answers questions about your studio from your real data.
Capabilities:
More prominent AI button — The floating AI button is easier to spot and lights up on hover.
Reliable replies — Fixed an issue where the AI Agent failed to respond.
Knows your whole studio — The AI now understands class management (templates, categories, ratings, rooms, branches), staff (members, pay rates, shifts, time off), studio settings (tax rates, agreements, cancellation and freeze policies, referrals, notifications), and customer plans and referrals.
"What's interesting" scan — A new tool scans the studio and surfaces the biggest signals right now.
Varied insights — Broad questions rotate across eight topics — Finance, Membership, Classes, Retail, Staff, Marketing, Lifecycle and Operations — so you get different angles instead of the same answer.
Helpful when there's no data — A brand-new studio with nothing set up yet gets a setup guide instead of empty charts.
Straight answers to specific questions — Ask for a specific detail and the AI answers it directly instead of only opening a profile card.

2 Customer Management
The studio's customer and lead list, with a full profile behind each one.
Capabilities:
Renaming a source stays clean — Rename a customer source and every record updates, with no leftover copies of the old name.
Honest lead-status messages — Marking a lead "Reached out" no longer claims it moves to Contacted when it's already past that stage.
Correct unassign log — Unassigning a customer now records "Unassigned customer" instead of "Reassigned customer".

3 Retail
The new module for selling retail products, taking shape now.
Capabilities:
Create and edit products — A full product form laid out in three parts: the steps on the left, the form in the middle, and a live preview on the right.
Live preview — The product card preview updates as you fill in the form.
Product detail page — Each product opens a detail page with a summary, stock by branch, and recent activity, organised into tabs with the actions kept in the sidebar.
Stock management — Receive new stock and adjust stock levels, kept accurate for each branch.

4 Staff & Shift
Managing staff members, their shifts, and time off.
Capabilities:
Side-panel creation — Adding or editing a staff member now opens in a side panel instead of a separate page, so you keep your place in the list.
Simpler button label — The "+ Add New" button now reads "+ Add".

5 Settings
Studio configuration — the referral programme, booking rules, and customer notifications.
Capabilities:
Tidier Referral layout — The Overview and Setup tabs now sit on the same row as the date filter, and the content fills the screen width.
Full-width pages — Booking Rules and Customer Notifications now fill the available width, so there's more room to read and edit.
