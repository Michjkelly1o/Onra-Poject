Onra Customer Prototype Update and Walkthrough
Customer modules touched: Search, Bookings, Home, Waitlist, Purchases
This update covers these modules:
Search
Bookings
Home
Waitlist
Purchases
Note:
This update runs across finding, booking, and buying. Search now splits into Classes, Private and Recovery tabs so you browse each kind of session on its own. Your bookings start empty on a fresh session and stay in step with the studio, private and recovery appointments can now be rated just like classes, and the home Upcoming bookings widget shows your next booking whatever its type. A full class reads "FULL" and, once you join the waitlist, shows your place in line, with a tidier waitlist checkout. And after any purchase you land on View purchase history. In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Search
Finding classes and appointments to book.
Capabilities:
- **Classes, Private and Recovery tabs** — Search is split into three tabs — Classes, Private and Recovery — so you browse each kind of session on its own.

2 Bookings
Your upcoming and past bookings, across classes and appointments.
Capabilities:
- **Empty upcoming on a fresh start** — A fresh session opens with no upcoming bookings, and your bookings stay in step with the studio's side.
- **Rate your appointments** — Private and recovery appointments can now be rated, the same way as classes.

3 Home
Your studio home, with your next booking front and centre.
Capabilities:
- **Next booking of any kind** — The Upcoming bookings widget now shows your next booking whether it's a class, a private, or a recovery session.

4 Waitlist
Joining the waitlist when a class is full.
Capabilities:
- **Clear "FULL" label** — A full class shows "FULL" instead of a waitlist count, so it's clear the class is full but you can still join the waitlist.
- **Your place in line** — After you join, your waitlist position shows — for example "Waitlist #2".
- **Cleaner waitlist checkout** — Removed the duplicate "Nothing is charged now" note; the waitlist notice already explains you're only charged once a spot opens up.

5 Purchases
Reviewing what you've bought.
Capabilities:
- **View purchase history** — After a purchase, the confirmation takes you to View purchase history, so it works the same for every kind of product.
