Customer modules touched: Home, Appointment
This update covers these modules:
Home
Appointment
Note:
This update focuses on booking an appointment. Instructor selection now opens with a "Preference: Flexible" option — let the studio assign an available instructor and the times you see are the combined openings of everyone qualified, so you only ever pick a slot someone can actually take. Your appointment also stays in step with the studio: it shows on their schedule the moment you book, updates if they reassign your instructor, and the appointment details now always show the location. In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Home
A clearer label on your studio stats.
Capabilities:
- **Upcoming classes** — The home stat that used to read "Classes remaining" now reads "Upcoming classes".

2 Appointment
Booking a private or recovery session, with a new flexible option.
Capabilities:
- **Preference: Flexible** — Instructor selection now starts with a "Preference: Flexible" option — let the studio assign an available instructor instead of choosing one yourself.
- **Times from every available instructor** — With Flexible chosen, the dates and times you see are the combined openings of all qualified instructors, so no available slot is hidden.
- **Only bookable slots appear** — A time shows only when at least one instructor is genuinely free for it, so you can never land on a slot with no one available.
- **An instructor is assigned for you** — When you book Flexible, the studio picks one of the available instructors automatically, and their name appears on your confirmation and booking.
- **Location always shown** — The appointment details now always show where the session is — the branch, plus the room when the service uses one.
- **In step with the studio** — Your appointment appears on the studio's schedule the moment you book; if they reassign your instructor your booking updates to match, and cancelling on either side keeps both in sync.
