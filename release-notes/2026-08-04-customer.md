Note:
This update reshapes booking and buying around slide-up panels — booking a class, a private or recovery session, or buying a product now happens in a panel that slides up over the screen, so you stay in place instead of jumping between pages. The home screen leads with your bookings and credits, keeps attended sessions in a new Previous bookings section, and puts Rate right on the booking card. Search reads cleaner, booking for a guest works end to end, and the whole app now renders at a tighter phone width. In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Home
Your studio home — your next booking, your credits, and a quick way to book.
Capabilities:
- **General booking stats** — The top counters now read in general terms — your bookings and credits left — and the counts stay correct as you book and cancel.
- **"Book" button** — The main action now simply reads "Book".
- **Clearer empty state** — When you have no upcoming bookings, the Upcoming section shows a friendlier, more helpful message.
- **Previous bookings** — A new Previous bookings section sits under Upcoming, showing sessions you've already attended — with its own empty state when there are none.
- **Rate from the card** — The Rate button now lives inside the booking card and uses the same compact button as the rest of the app.

2 Search
Finding classes, private and recovery sessions to book.
Capabilities:
- **Calmer participant badge** — The participant count on each class now shows as a soft grey badge, so it no longer competes with the class details.
- **Book for a guest** — You can now book a session for a guest — including booking for a guest only — the way Mariana Tek does it.
- **Static month, current week onward** — The month now shows as plain text with no picker to open, and the week strip only moves forward from the current week — across Classes, Private and Recovery.

3 Class Booking
Booking a class, from browsing through to payment.
Capabilities:
- **Details first, then Book now** — Tapping a class always opens Class Details first; the action reads "Book now" and opens Review & Book from either entry point.
- **Review & Book panel** — Review & Book is now a slide-up panel with a tidier class summary, a single "Book to" section that combines the guest choice and spot picking, and the familiar sticky booking footer.
- **Guest details** — Booking for a guest opens a Guest Details panel you can edit, and you can switch freely between Myself and Guest.
- **Smarter payment options** — Eligible plans are pre-selected for you, "Purchase Product" now reads "Purchase plan", the purchase step opens as a slide-up panel, and the guest payment options are updated.

4 Private & Recovery
Booking a one-to-one or recovery session.
Capabilities:
- **Slide-up booking steps** — Choosing your instructor and your date & time now happen in slide-up panels that flow straight into checkout, with a Back step at every stage.
- **Better date availability** — The date list opens on the current week and lands on the first day that actually has openings.
- **Matches the Classes tab** — The static month and current-week-onward strip behave exactly like the Classes tab.

5 Products
Browsing and buying memberships, packages and gift cards.
Capabilities:
- **Product details panel** — Product details now open as a slide-up panel, with Pay now as the main action and Add to cart alongside it. Memberships no longer show a quantity selector.
- **Checkout as a panel** — The cart, payment, and promo steps open as connected slide-up panels, so buying stays on one screen.
- **"Gift Cards"** — The gift cards tab is now correctly plural.
- **Custom-amount gift card** — A custom-amount gift card is now in the catalogue, so you can try filling in your own amount end to end.
- **Tidier back navigation** — Leaving the Products area now returns you where you expect.

6 Profile
Your plan, credits, and saved gift cards.
Capabilities:
- **Renews on / Expires on** — Your credit balance now shows "Renews on" for a membership and "Expires on" for a credit package, so it's clear what happens next.
- **"Gift cards"** — The gift cards section is now correctly plural.

General updates
- **Phone-width canvas** — The customer app now renders at a tighter, more phone-accurate width for a truer preview.
- **Full experience audit** — Booking, appointment, and purchase flows were reviewed end to end for navigation, business rules, responsive behaviour, and design consistency after the redesign.
