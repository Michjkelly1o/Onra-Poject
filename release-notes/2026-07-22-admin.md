Onra Admin Prototype Update and Walkthrough
Overall modules done 15/15
This update covers these modules:
Dashboard
Class Schedule
Private sessions & Recovery & wellness
Marketing & Campaigns
Customer Notifications
Settings — Freeze policy
Staff & Shift

Note:
This push lands the Staff & Shift rework across all three tabs, a full logic pass on the Freeze policy, several Dashboard fixes and new widgets, and a sweep to standardise every list toolbar. It also carries the "Members → Customers" wording change across every admin and instructor screen. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Dashboard
The studio's at-a-glance home — revenue, bookings, class activity, and now referral performance in one place.
Capabilities:
Date & heatmap fixes — Filtering by month now works correctly across the dashboard, and the activity heatmap reads the right days.
Referral chart — A new performance widget shows how referrals are trending alongside the other studio metrics.
Info tips on widgets — Selected widgets now carry a small "i" you can hover for a plain-language explanation of what the number means.
Calendar day overflow — When a day shows "+17 more", selecting it opens a modal listing every class that day; picking one opens that class's details.

2 Class Schedule
Create and manage classes, private sessions, and recovery & wellness bookings.
Capabilities:
Split home filters — Class type and location are now separate filters, so you can narrow by one without touching the other.
Delete the first timeslot — In the schedule creation flow (step 3), the first timeslot can now be removed, and doing so drops that day from the selection.
Toolbar order — Every list toolbar follows one consistent order: All locations, then search, export, filter, and the primary action button.

3 Private sessions & Recovery & wellness
Standalone booking flows for one-to-one and wellness sessions.
Capabilities:
Add new restored — The "Add new" button is back on both the Private sessions and Recovery & wellness modules.

4 Marketing & Campaigns
Build and schedule promotions and announcements for members.
Capabilities:
Correct image ratio — Campaign images now use the same aspect ratio as the customer side, so pictures are no longer cut off or cropped in the admin view.

5 Customer Notifications
Manage the studio's outbound notification templates and delivery rules.
Capabilities:
Sticky table header — The column header now stays fixed at the top as you scroll the list.

6 Settings — Freeze policy
Control how members pause their memberships.
Capabilities:
Corrected logic — The underlying freeze behaviour was reworked so fees, penalties, expiry dates, and booking blocks all behave correctly and consistently, whether a freeze is started by staff or by the customer.
Rolling 12 months — The freeze limit now counts over a rolling 12-month window instead of a fixed calendar year.
Cleaner copy — Removed the extra helper lines under the duration and freeze-limit settings; the titles alone now carry the meaning.
Customers, not members — Every freeze-policy screen (and related pages) now says "Customers" instead of "Members".

7 Staff & Shift
Manage staff, their shift assignments, and time off.
Capabilities:
Three-tab rework — The Staff, Shifts, and Time off tabs were all updated: staffing targets with an understaffed indicator, staff who can hold more than one shift, a read-only week view of assigned shifts, and a month calendar of time off with an overlap flag.
Multi-day time off — Time off now covers a date range with a reason category, and a range that spans several days correctly blocks every day it covers across scheduling and booking.

General updates
Consistent toolbar order — Across admin and instructor, every module/page toolbar follows the same order: All locations, search, export, filter, then the primary action.
Filter icon — The filter icon is now an up/down control everywhere it appears.
Customers wording — "Members" now reads "Customers" across all admin and instructor screens.
Verified freeze & staff modules — Both the Freeze policy and Staff & Shift modules were reviewed end-to-end to confirm the data, actions, and cross-screen updates all hold together.
