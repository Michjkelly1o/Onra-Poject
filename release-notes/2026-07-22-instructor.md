1 Shifts & Time off
The instructor's view of their own shifts and the time off they've requested.
Capabilities:
Matches the new Staff & Shift model — Shifts and time off now follow the same updated model as the admin side, including support for an instructor holding more than one shift.
Multi-day time off — A single time-off entry can span a date range, and it correctly blocks every day it covers in the instructor's schedule.
Own entries only — Time off an instructor submits is theirs to manage; entries the studio created for a group show as read-only with a note that they're managed by admin.
Customers wording — Instructor screens now say "Customers" instead of "Members".
