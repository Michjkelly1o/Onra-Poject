Note:
This update reworks guest booking and buying around the studio's plans and stock. Adding a guest now uses their phone number, keeps just two ways to pay — your own plan or a WhatsApp invite link — and the invited guest signs in and finishes the booking themselves. If you don't hold an eligible plan, you can now pay for a class with your wallet or another method, or buy a new plan without leaving Review & Book. Products splits memberships and packages into their own tabs, Retail asks you to pick a pickup branch and keeps its stock in step with the studio, and Class Details now shows Gender. In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Home
Your studio home — your next booking, your credits, and a quick way to book.
Capabilities:
Past Booking — The "Previous Booking" section is now called "Past Booking", to keep the wording consistent across the app.

2 Class Booking
Book a class, add a guest, and pay the way that suits you.
Capabilities:
Gender in Class Details — Class Details now shows Gender, placed right after Class Capacity and before Class Type, in the same layout as the other details.
Guest by phone number — Adding a guest now asks for their phone number instead of their email, and the guest's details in the booking read the same way.
Simpler guest payment — A guest booking keeps just two options: pay with your own plan when that's allowed, or send an invite link.
Invite by WhatsApp — The invite link goes out over WhatsApp; the guest signs in or creates an account and completes the booking themselves.
Pay without an eligible plan — If none of your plans cover the class, you can pay with your wallet or another method, or buy a new plan — all from Review & Book.
Buy a plan in place — The purchase-a-plan steps now open as slide-up panels connected to Review & Book, so you stay in the same flow.

3 Products
Buy memberships, packages and gift cards.
Capabilities:
Memberships and Packages split out — Memberships and Packages now have their own tabs, the tabs scroll sideways when there are many, and the two are shown as separate sections under the All tab.

4 Retail
Buy studio retail products for pickup.
Capabilities:
Pick a pickup branch first — Buying a retail product now asks you to choose the branch you'll pick it up from before you check out.
Out-of-stock guidance — When a size or variant is sold out at your branch but available at another, the product points you to the branch where you can get it.
Stock stays in step — Retail stock always matches the studio's real counts, whether you're viewing All Branches or a single branch.
Correct stock per size — Each product size shows the stock for the branch you've selected and updates the moment you switch branches.

5 My Plan
Your plan, credits, and saved gift cards.
Capabilities:
Per-package expiry — A helper icon next to "Expires on" opens a short sheet listing the expiry date for each of your credit packages when you hold more than one.

6 About
The studio's About page.
Capabilities:
Updated brand icon — The About page now shows the latest Onra brand icon.

General updates
Guests become studio contacts — When you add a guest to a booking, their details are saved to the studio's contacts as a new lead, so the studio can follow up with them.
