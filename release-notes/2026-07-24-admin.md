Onra Admin Prototype Update and Walkthrough
Overall modules done 15/15
This update covers these modules:
Dashboard
Insights
AI Agent
Staff & Shifts
Schedule
Appointments

Note:
This push is a big step up for the Onra AI Agent — it now brings your existing data in end to end, answers more precisely, asks its questions once, and can hand back a downloadable report. Alongside that: the Insights screen is reworked across every tab, Staff & Shifts gains branch- and role-aware shift assignment with Week-calendar management, the Schedule follows the real date and time, and appointments booked on the customer app now show up here the moment they happen. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Dashboard
The owner's home screen — the studio's key numbers at a glance.
Capabilities:
Payments overview colour coding — Collected payments now read in green and failed payments in red, so the healthy-versus-problem split is obvious at a glance.

2 Insights
The studio's performance view, across six tabs — Financial, Customer, Classes, Private sessions, Recovery and Marketing.
Capabilities:
Reworked metrics on every tab — The tiles on all six tabs have been restructured to match the latest feedback, so each number is grouped and labelled the way the studio reads it.
Numbers follow your date range — Figures like active customers, subscriptions and intro offers now update with the period you pick instead of staying fixed.
Filters on the tab row — The Location filter, Search and Date Period selector now sit on the same row as the tabs, matching the Dashboard layout, and the tab strip stays pinned as you scroll.

3 AI Agent
A chat assistant that helps you set up the studio, find answers, and bring in your existing data.
Capabilities:
Import your data, guided — Bring in your existing records — customers, classes, bookings, gift cards, payroll and more — right inside the chat: it detects the branch, previews the columns, lets you map them, and imports.
Export and download — Ask for a report and the agent hands back a downloadable file, as CSV or Excel, directly in the conversation.
Clearer, sharper answers — Responses are more precise and skip the filler, so you get the point without the back-and-forth.
Asks once — Follow-up questions no longer repeat or stack; the agent gathers what it needs in one place and moves on, including at export.
Question-and-answer recap — Your replies show as clean question-and-answer pairs, so the thread stays easy to follow.
Cards open in a new tab — Tapping a result — an insight, a customer, a schedule — opens it in a new tab, so you keep your place in the chat.
Connected to your studio — The agent reads and acts on your live studio data, so what it shows and creates lines up with the rest of the admin.

4 Staff & Shifts
Manage staff, their shifts, and time off across every location.
Capabilities:
Assign a shift from any staff form — The Assign Shift step now appears on every staff create and edit form, not just for instructors.
More than one shift per person — A staff member can hold several shifts at once; assigning one adds to their shifts rather than replacing them.
Shifts stay in the right location — The shift picker only offers shifts from the staff member's own branch, and a staffer can't be placed on another branch's shift.
Shifts respect the role — Assignable shifts follow the staff member's role, for every role except Owner.
Shifts follow branch hours — When you create a shift, its days and hours follow the selected branch's operating days and hours.
Working hours and pay rate on the profile — Every staff detail now shows the person's working hours and their default pay rate.
Manage shifts from the Week view — Each card has an Unassign action with a confirmation and an Assign action filtered by branch, operating day, and non-overlapping times.
Assign or remove a shift for one day — You can add or drop a shift on a single day without changing the rest of the week.
View Schedule opens filtered — The View Schedule action opens the Schedule already narrowed to that instructor.
Filters per tab — Each Staff & Shifts tab carries its own set of filters.

5 Schedule
The studio calendar for classes and appointments.
Capabilities:
Follows the real date and time — The schedule now uses the current device date and time, so "today" is genuinely today.
Scrollable instructor header in Day view — The Day view's instructor header scrolls sideways, so studios with a long list of instructors can reach everyone.

6 Appointments
Private and recovery sessions, managed from the Schedule and Appointment Details.
Capabilities:
Customer bookings show up here — An appointment booked on the customer app now appears on the Schedule and opens in Appointment Details, in real time.
Flexible tag — A private appointment booked with "Preference: Flexible" — where the studio picks the instructor — is marked with a Flexible tag so it's easy to spot.
Reassign the instructor — For a Flexible appointment you can reassign the instructor straight from Appointment Details, and the change carries back to the customer's booking.

General updates
"Staff & Shifts" naming — The sidebar entry and headings now read "Staff & Shifts".
AI Agent in the sidebar — A quick entry to the AI Agent sits in the sidebar footer, directly above Settings.
