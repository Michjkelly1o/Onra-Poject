Onra Customer Prototype Update and Walkthrough
Customer modules touched: Appointments, Payment History, Checkout & Payment
This update covers these modules:
Appointments
Payment History
Checkout & Payment

Note:
This update keeps your payments and appointments in step with the studio. Booking a private or recovery session now finishes with a "View payment history" button, and its invoice shows straight away in Payment history — the same record the studio sees. Appointments the studio books for you at the front desk now appear in your app too, "View purchase history" is renamed "View payment history" to match, and the payment methods you're offered mirror the ones the studio has switched on. In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Appointments
Book a private or recovery session.
Capabilities:
View payment history after booking — The booking-confirmed screen for both private and recovery sessions now has a "View payment history" button, so you can jump straight to the invoice for what you just booked.
Studio-booked appointments show up — An appointment the studio books for you at the front desk now appears in your app, kept in sync with the studio's records.

2 Payment History
Your invoices and payments.
Capabilities:
Renamed to "View payment history" — "View purchase history" is now "View payment history", matching what the studio calls it, so the name is consistent on both sides.
Appointment invoices appear here — Booking a private or recovery session adds its invoice to Payment history right away, and it stays in sync with the studio's record.

3 Checkout & Payment
Pay for a class, an appointment, or a product.
Capabilities:
Payment methods match the studio — You're only offered the payment methods the studio has switched on — turn off Apple Pay or Google Pay on the studio side and it no longer shows here.
