Onra Admin Prototype Update and Walkthrough
Overall modules done 16/16
This update covers these modules:
Point of Sale
Products
Settings
Attendees
Reports
Dashboard

Note:
The front desk can now sell private and recovery sessions right from the till — picking the date, instructor, and time books the appointment and takes payment in one step — and the whole product catalog is now one clean, scrollable list. A new Services page in Settings lets a studio switch off what it doesn't offer (Memberships, Private sessions, Recovery), and those same switches appear during onboarding. There's a brand-new Attendees role with its own phone app that shows only today's classes for checking people in. The Dashboard gains a Revenue & new customers chart, and the Revenue recognition report now tracks unused credits correctly. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Point of Sale
The front-desk till, now selling private and recovery sessions and easier to scan.
Capabilities:
Sell private and recovery sessions — The catalog now has Private sessions and Recovery tabs alongside Memberships & packages, Retail, and Gift cards.
Book while you sell — Choosing a private or recovery session opens a quick panel to pick the date, instructor, and time; completing the sale books the appointment and takes payment together.
Right tax on each — Private and recovery are taxed separately, so checkout always applies the correct rate.
One clean list — The whole catalog is now a single scrollable list of rows — image, name, details, and price — instead of a grid of cards.
Familiar cart icon — The shopping-bag icon now matches the one in the member app.

2 Products
The products area, tidied up and renamed to match the rest of the app.
Capabilities:
Simpler menu name — "Products & pricing" in the side menu is now just "Products".
"Packages" everywhere — "Credit packages" now reads simply "Packages" across the Memberships & Packages screens.
"Location" column — The Memberships list column "Branch location" is now "Location", matching every other screen.
Less to fill in — The welcome-message box is gone from the new-product form; that message already lives in Customer notifications.
Collapsible branch pricing — When a retail product comes in sizes, each branch's stock in step 2 is now a section you can open and close, so a studio with many branches can hide the ones it isn't editing.
Tidy categories — The leftover "Recovery" retail category, used only for testing, was removed to avoid confusion.

3 Settings
Studio-wide configuration, now including which services your studio actually offers.
Capabilities:
Turn services on or off — A new Services page lets you switch off what you don't sell — Memberships, Private sessions, or Recovery — and everything tied to it hides across menus, the till, customer plans, and reports.
Confirm before each change — Turning a service on or off asks you to confirm first, so nothing is hidden or brought back by accident.
Shown during onboarding — New studios see these switches while setting up, so they only turn on what they offer.
Clearer cancellation label — In Customer notifications, "Cancellation confirm" is now "Cancellation confirmation".
Separate private and recovery tax — The single "Appointment" tax is now split into its own Private and Recovery rates, so each is charged correctly at checkout.

4 Attendees
A brand-new role and a dedicated attendance app for staff who only check people in.
Capabilities:
New Attendees role — A new role in Staff & Permissions, with a permission set matched to just marking class attendance and nothing else.
Their own app — Anyone assigned this role signs in to a simple, phone-friendly app made only for taking attendance.
Classes only — The app shows classes only; private and recovery sessions don't appear.
Today's classes, grouped — Classes are split into Ongoing and Upcoming. A class moves to Ongoing 30 minutes before it starts; everything else scheduled for today sits under Upcoming.
Quick to scan — The list uses smaller images and a compact row layout, so a full day fits on a phone screen.
No clutter — The class search was removed; the day's list is short enough to read straight down.
Cleaner admin side — With the app in place, the old attendee tabs and shortcuts were removed from the admin Schedule header, the Dashboard's Today view, and the class, private, and recovery detail pages.

5 Reports
Fixes to credit tracking and exports.
Capabilities:
Remaining credits now correct — On the Revenue recognition report, the Remaining column shows the credits still unused — a fresh 20-credit sale now reads 20, not 0.
Cleaner time labels — A single month now reads "1 month" instead of "1 months".
Fixed payroll export — The Basis column in the exported payroll file now fills in correctly instead of coming out blank.

6 Dashboard
The owner's home view, with a new revenue-and-growth chart.
Capabilities:
Revenue & new customers — A new chart under the Financial tab plots revenue over time against how many customers are new to the studio, and follows your location and date filters.

General updates
One name for Recovery — The app now says "Recovery" everywhere, instead of sometimes "Recovery & wellness" — one consistent name.
Assistant button stays behind pop-ups — The floating assistant button now sits behind any open window or side panel, so it never covers what you're working on.
