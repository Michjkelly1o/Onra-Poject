Note:
This update makes retail pickup and appointment booking clearer. Retail items in your cart now show where you'll collect them — "Collection — Forma Studio (South)" — and your order note reminds you to show the order at the front desk. A retail order stays to a single branch, and your purchase history matches the studio's records. Picking an appointment time now skips days with no openings and lands you on the next available day, the single-class package is a clean 1 credit for 1 class, and "Invite friends" is now "Refer a friend". In the prototype, you may still see a 404 on a page — that means the page/module/feature is still being developed. To navigate to the customer side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/customer"; for the admin side use "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard".
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Checkout & Payment
Pay for a class, an appointment, or a product.
Capabilities:
Collection branch on cart lines — A retail item in the cart now shows "Collection — Forma Studio (South)" — your chosen pickup branch — in place of the stock count, and a sized item reads "Size M • Collection — {branch}".
Front-desk reminder on the order — Your order details now include "Show this order at the front desk", so you know what to do when you arrive.

2 Retail
Buy studio retail products for pickup.
Capabilities:
One branch per order — A retail order can only contain products from the same branch, so everything in it is collected from one place.
Purchase history stays in sync — Your retail purchase history always matches the studio's records.

3 Products
Buy memberships, packages and gift cards.
Capabilities:
Single-class package — The single-class package is now a clean 1 credit for 1 class, with its name, description and credits all matching.

4 Appointments
Book a private or recovery session.
Capabilities:
Smarter date and time picking — Days with no openings are greyed out, and the picker jumps straight to the next available day instead of opening on an empty one.

5 Profile
Your account, plan and settings.
Capabilities:
"Refer a friend" — The "Invite friends" option is now "Refer a friend", on both the menu and the page header.
