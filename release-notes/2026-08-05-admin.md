Note:
This update reshapes how you manage people and time. Staff and Shift are now two separate areas: Staff has a Staff list and a Staff schedule, with a today-at-a-glance bar on every row; Shift keeps a simple shift list and a Time Off list. Time Off now flows straight into the calendar — booking an instructor off automatically cancels their upcoming classes and refunds the members. Tax is simplified to one flat 5% VAT across everything to match UAE. Reports, POS, the Dashboard and Customer Management all get a round of clean-ups, and the AI Agent can now build a class template for you. In the prototype you may still see some 404 pages; that means the page/module/feature is still being developed.
New side for admin: to navigate to admin please change the link to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/admin/dashboard" and for the instructor side, please change it back to "https://onra-poject.vercel.app/instructor/dashboard"
Interactive experience — This is a fully interactive prototype. Feel free to click, create, edit, and delete items to test the workflows.
Live updates — The prototype uses realistic sample data. Any changes you make apply live as you navigate between different screens.
Session persistence — Any data you create, edit, or cancel survives a page refresh and tab close. Your demo session sticks until you explicitly reset.

1 Staff
The studio's people list, now its own area separate from shifts.
Capabilities:
Staff and Shift split apart — Staff and Shift are now two independent areas, each with its own tabs, so managing people and managing time no longer overlap.
Two tabs — Staff has a Staff list tab and a Staff schedule tab.
Today's schedule at a glance — Every staff row shows a small bar of today's sessions with the shift name and time, and a Time Off marker on the days they're off.
Tap through to the schedule — Tapping an instructor's today's schedule opens the Schedule with that instructor already filtered in, so you land straight on their day.

2 Shift
Where you set up shifts and record time off.
Capabilities:
Two tabs — Shift has a Shift tab and a Time off tab.
Simpler shift list — The shift list now shows just the shift's own details; the staffing-count column and the assigned-staff day picker are hidden to keep it clean.
Time Off as a list — The Time Off calendar view is hidden; time off reads as a straightforward list instead.

3 Schedule
The studio calendar across day, week and month.
Capabilities:
Focused day view — Filtering the day view to one instructor shows only that instructor, and their column fills the whole width instead of hugging the left side.
Time Off naming — "Blocked time" is now called "Time Off" throughout the schedule.
Time Off cancels classes automatically — Booking an instructor off cancels their upcoming classes in that window and refunds each member's class credit automatically.
Consistent cancel and refund — Cancelling a class from the schedule uses the same confirmation with the credit-refund option you see everywhere else.
No "FULL" on private sessions — Private and recovery cards no longer show "1/1 (FULL)"; a one-to-one session is always full, so the tag only added noise.

4 Dashboard
The studio's at-a-glance overview.
Capabilities:
Consistent revenue tooltip — The revenue tile's explanation now matches the one on the Insights page, so the two read the same.
Cleaner today's sessions — Private and recovery sessions drop the "1/1 (FULL)" tag on Today's sessions.

5 Reports
Pull the numbers behind sales, attendance and more.
Capabilities:
Export button matches the rest — The export button is now the same compact icon button used across the other modules.
Clean export values — Fixed the stray "—" characters that were showing up in some export cells.
Removed the unused Measure control — The Measure dropdown didn't change anything on the report, so it's been removed.
Sales by Category — This report now groups by revenue category only, dropping the location grouping that wasn't needed.
Cleaner sales rows — Sale items no longer show a redundant "x1" (quantity has its own column), and the sales channel now reads simply Online or POS.

6 Point of Sale
Take payment for memberships, packages, gift cards and retail.
Capabilities:
Auto-filled staff member — The logged-in staff member is filled in automatically as the person handling the sale, so it doesn't have to be picked each time.
Payment as a side panel — Taking payment now opens in a side panel, matching the branding side panel in Settings, so checkout stays in place.

7 Settings
Studio-wide configuration, including tax.
Capabilities:
One flat 5% VAT — Every service and retail item is taxed at a single 5% VAT to match UAE, so there's no need to set tax per item — the per-category tax tab is hidden and every sale still charges 5%.
Gift cards taxed on use — Gift cards aren't taxed when bought; tax applies when they're spent, at the rate of whatever they're spent on.
No payroll tax for UAE — The Income tax tab only appears for markets that charge payroll tax, so it's hidden for Dubai/UAE.
Tidier tax list — The "Pay rate tax" row is removed from the Tax rates list for UAE; it returns automatically for an income-tax market.

8 Customer Management
The studio's customer and lead list.
Capabilities:
Steady table columns — The Contact column keeps a fixed width, so sorting the customer table no longer shifts or resizes the columns.

9 AI Agent
A chat assistant that helps you set things up and get answers.
Capabilities:
Assistant bar on the right — The floating assistant bar now sits on the right of the screen.
Create a class template by chat — The assistant can now build a class template for you from the conversation.
Smoother publish — Fixed the loading screen shown after publishing a class so it completes cleanly.
